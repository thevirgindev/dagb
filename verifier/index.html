DAGB QR Scanner

Scan Discord's age verification QR code with your camera. The scanner will extract the verification link and open it.

Starting camera...
  1. Open Discord and start age verification until you see the QR code on screen.
  2. Point your phone camera (or this page on another device) at the QR code.
  3. The scanner will detect the code and show the verification link.
  4. Tap "Open verification link" to complete the age check.
  5. Once verified, your Discord account is marked as adult server-side.
